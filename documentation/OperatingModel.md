# The Operating Model (Canonical)

This is the internal logic that governs the entire system.
Every feature, guide, and tool must cleanly map to one part of this model.

The operating model explains:

* How problems form
* How change happens
* What stabilizes the change
* What the system must enforce at every level

Here is the clean, professional version.

---

# The Quality Operating Model

## 1. The Premise

Life quality emerges from the interaction of three layers working in harmony:

1. **Worldview**
2. **Frameworks**
3. **Systems in daily life**

Misalignment in any layer generates gumption traps, chaos, or stagnation.
Alignment generates clarity, flow, and upward spirals.

This is the baseline ontology of the universe.

---

## 2. The State Machine

Every user sits somewhere in a five state cycle:

**Friction → Trap → Pattern → Redesign → Stabilization**

The system’s job is to:

* Detect where they are
* Show them the correct next action
* Prevent regressions
* Promote compounding returns

This is not a moral journey. It is an engineering sequence.

---

## 3. The Mechanism of Change

Every successful shift follows the same mechanism:

### A. Tension

User feels the mismatch between desired life and actual systems.

### B. Diagnosis

We identify the gumption trap:

* Friction overload
* Ambiguity
* Lack of cues
* Environmental sabotage
* Overcomplexity
* Undefined standards
* Misaligned incentives

### C. Redesign

We modify the system, not the person.

This includes:

* Environment
* Workflow
* Constraints
* Cues
* Routines
* Visual controls
* Feedback loops

### D. Gumption Recovery

The user experiences renewed energy, clarity, and agency.

### E. Static Latch

The improvement becomes part of their stable baseline.

### F. Return to Dynamic Growth

Their capacity for creative action and further improvement increases.

This mechanism must be visible in all tools.

---

## 4. The Responsibilities of the System

The system itself must guarantee:

1. **No shame framing**
   All friction is treated as a systems problem.

2. **Immediate pattern recognition**
   Users must see their struggle as predictable, not personal.

3. **Single-lever interventions**
   No overwhelm. One lever at a time.

4. **Rapid early wins**
   The first redesign must deliver relief within 24 to 72 hours.

5. **Compounding clarity**
   Each win should make the next win more likely.

6. **Layer discipline**
   Tools never rewrite worldview.
   Worldview never tries to act like a tool.

7. **User agency above all**
   Every mechanism must increase the user’s sense of authorship, not dependency.

---

## 5. The Role of the Practitioner

The user is positioned as:

* Observer of patterns
* Diagnostician of their own traps
* Designer of systems
* Steward of Quality
* Apprentice in the craft of life

Tools exist to support their craft, not to dominate their behavior.

---

## 6. The Operating Model as a Flow

Here is the simplest working version:

**Signal → Diagnosis → Lever → Redesign → Stabilize → Iterate**

The system’s job is to keep the user cycling through this loop with decreasing friction and increasing agency.

This loop is the beating heart of the entire project.

---

# How the Entry Point and Operating Model Fit Together

The Gumption Trap Analyzer:

* Delivers the first **Signal → Diagnosis**
* Offers the first **Lever**
* Initiates the first **Redesign**
* Produces the first **Stabilization**
* Unlocks the loop

From there, everything else in the ecosystem simply continues the cycle at higher resolution.

This makes the Analyzer not just an entry point, but the perfect microcosm of the entire Operating Model.
